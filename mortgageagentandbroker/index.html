In Ontario, the Financial Services Regulatory Authority of Ontario (FSRA) establishes a clear three-tier career progression hierarchy for mortgage professionals. The fundamental difference lies in the types of lenders they can access and their level of supervisory and operational authority.

📌 1. Mortgage Agent Level 1
This is the starting point for anyone entering the mortgage industry in Ontario. Under this license, you are strictly limited to institutional "A" lending spaces. If a client has poor credit or is self-employed and requires alternative or private financing, a Level 1 agent cannot complete the deal independently; they must hand the transaction over to a Level 2 Agent or a Broker.
💡 2. Mortgage Agent Level 2
This tier was introduced to protect consumers entering the volatile private lending market. Moving to Level 2 opens up the entire market, letting you work with Alternative "B" lenders, Mortgage Investment Corporations (MICs), and private investors. It is highly valuable if you plan to deal with non-traditional borrowers or property investors.
🌟 3. Mortgage Broker
A Mortgage Broker holds the most senior license designation in Ontario. Beyond having total freedom over which lenders they utilize, brokers are legally allowed to manage business operations. Only a licensed broker can supervise agents, act as a brokerage compliance manager (Principal Broker), or launch an independent mortgage brokerage firm.

Barrie Population Density and Geography

User prompt: Which part of the city of Barrie, Ontario has the highest population density? Is the north part more densely populated, or the south part of Barrie? How is the city divided geographically?

Response: The central and northern parts of Barrie are the most densely populated, while the southern end is the least dense.

Geographically, Barrie is divided into four main quadrants by two major landmarks:

  • Kempenfelt Bay (Lake Simcoe): Cuts deeply into the city from the east, creating a natural North/South boundary.
  • Highway 400: Runs straight through the city, creating an East/West divide.

These quadrants contain over 30 distinct neighborhoods, which the city politically organizes into 10 wards.

North vs. South Population Density

While Barrie's southern wards have experienced rapid population growth and contain some of the highest total populations in the city, they also cover massive geographic areas. Because the central and northern wards are older and more established, they pack a similar number of residents into a fraction of the space.

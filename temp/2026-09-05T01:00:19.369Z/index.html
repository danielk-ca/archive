Shopping Tips for Liquidation Stores

  • Test Before Buying: Bin and open-box stores usually sell items as-is; always make sure to use their in-store testing stations before checking out.

  • Visit on Restock Days: For bin-style stores like Crown Binz or TopBinz, arriving on restocking days gives you the highest quality selection, while visiting later in the week offers the lowest prices.

Top Alternatives Breakdown
  • SONOFF iFan04-L / iFan04-H: A budget-friendly Wi-Fi module that fits inside the canopy and provides independent control of the light and a 3-speed AC fan motor. It comes with a 433MHz RF handheld/wall remote and connects natively to Google Home and Amazon Alexa. It does not natively support local-only open-source platforms like Home Assistant without flashing or specific integrations, but it is inexpensive and widely available.
  • Insteon FanLinc (2475F)
    Go to product viewer dialog for this item.
    : A robust dual-band in-ceiling receiver that controls fan speeds (High, Medium, Low, Off) and offers full dimming for the light kit. It requires an Insteon network controller, keypad, or the Insteon Hub to interface with modern smart home systems.
  • Bond Bridge (Alternative Approach): If your fan already uses an RF or IR remote control, keeping the stock receiver in the canopy and pairing it with a Bond Bridge device allows local and cloud remote control of both fan speed and light via Wi-Fi, Alexa, Google Home, and Home Assistant without rewiring the canopy.

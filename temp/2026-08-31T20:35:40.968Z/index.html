Split a single wired 2.5 Gbps port into multiple connections

Author: Google Gemini

To split a single 2.5 Gbps port from your ISP combo into multiple wired 2.5 Gbps connections for your NAS, home server, and other devices, you need an unmanaged or managed 2.5 Gbps Ethernet switch .

How It Works

  1. Plug an Ethernet cable from the 2.5 Gbps LAN port on your ISP modem/router combo into the uplink port of a 2.5 Gbps switch.
  2. Plug your NAS, home server, and other wired gear directly into the remaining 2.5 Gbps ports on the switch.
  3. All connected local devices will communicate with each other at full 2.5 Gbps speeds , and they will share the available 2.5 Gbps internet bandwidth .

Important Things to Keep in Mind

  • Internal vs. Internet Speed: Your individual devices will get 2.5 Gbps local transfer speeds when talking to your NAS or server, but they will split or share the max 2.5 Gbps internet pool when downloading from the web at the same time.
  • Cabling: Make sure you use Cat5e or Cat6 cables (Cat6 is preferred for multi-gig stability over longer home runs) to guarantee a solid 2.5 Gbps link.
  • Device Requirements: Ensure your NAS and home server actually have 2.5 Gbps network cards (NICs) installed, or add USB/PCIe 2.5 Gbps adapters if they only have standard 1 Gbps ports.

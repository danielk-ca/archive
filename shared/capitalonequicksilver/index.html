Capital One Quicksilver Mastercard: World Elite vs World

• Capital One Quicksilver World Elite Mastercard
• Capital One Quicksilver World Mastercard

Both the Capital One Quicksilver World Elite Mastercard and the Capital One Quicksilver World Mastercard are $0-annual-fee travel and cash back rewards cards with 0% foreign transaction fees.

The primary differences lie in their income requirements, interest rates, and Mastercard tier level.

Key Similarities

  • Annual Fee: $0 on both cards.

  • Foreign Exchange (FX) Fees: 0% FX fees on purchases made outside Canada.

  • Cash Back Rewards:

    • Unlimited 3% cash back on gas and EV charging stations, and grocery store purchases.

    • Unlimited 1% cash back on all other purchases.

  • First-Year Offer: An unlimited dollar-for-dollar cash back match at the end of the first 365 days (effectively doubling cash back to 6% on gas/groceries and 2% everywhere else for the first year).

  • Insurance & Protection Benefits: Both cards include identical suites of everyday, travel, and purchase protections (e.g., Purchase Assurance, Extended Warranty, Car Rental Collision/Loss Damage Insurance, Price Protection Service, Common Carrier Travel Accident Insurance, Flight Delay Insurance, Baggage Delay Insurance, and Lost Baggage Insurance).

Advantages of the World Elite Tier

The advantages of the World Elite tier come down to Mastercard network-level perks and account terms:

  1. Lower, Fixed Interest Rate

    • World Elite: Features a flat, lower interest rate of 20.9% across purchases, balance transfers, and cash advances.

    • World: Features a higher, variable interest rate ranging from 21.9% to 29.9%

  2. World Elite Concierge Service

    • World Elite cardholders receive complimentary 24/7 personal concierge assistance for booking travel, arranging restaurant reservations, securing event tickets, and managing lifestyle requests—a feature not included with standard World Mastercards.

  3. Enhanced Network Perks and Partner Discounts

    • The World Elite tier unlocks higher-tier Mastercard network privileges, including exclusive access to curated "Priceless" experiences, enhanced merchant discounts (such as travel services, entertainment offers, and lifestyle promotions), and better statement credit offers with partner brands (like Booking.com, H&R Block, and Fubo).

  4. Tier Exclusivity (Higher Qualification Bar)

    • While structured as an eligibility requirement rather than a direct perk, the World Elite tier positions the card for higher earners—requiring a minimum individual income of $80,000 (or a $150,000 household income) compared to $50,000 individual / $80,000 household for the World version.

If you qualify for the income threshold, choosing the World Elite version is objectively better because it offers a lower interest rate and additional concierge benefits for the exact same $0 annual fee.

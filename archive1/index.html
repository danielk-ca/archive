User prompt: Largest Indian Pharmaceutical Manufacturers of Dapoxetine in its finished commercial form (Example: Poxet by Sunrise Remedies Pvt. Ltd.)?

Response: India is one of the world's primary producers of finished pharmaceutical formulations of Dapoxetine (a fast-acting SSRI indicated for premature ejaculation). Commercial finished forms are manufactured as both standalone single-active tablets (typically 30 mg, 60 mg, or 90 mg) and fixed-dose combinations (commonly paired with Sildenafil, Tadalafil, or Avanafil).

The market is split between tier-1 mainstream Indian multinational pharmaceutical corporations (domestic prescription market) and specialty formulation/export leaders (international and specialty retail supply).

1. Mainstream & Tier-1 Indian Pharma Giants

These are large, publicly listed or major multinational Indian pharmaceutical companies with extensive domestic distribution across India:

  • Sun Pharmaceutical Industries Ltd. (India's largest pharmaceutical company)

    • Flagship Brand: Duralast (30 mg and 60 mg tablets)

  • Intas Pharmaceuticals Ltd.

    • Flagship Brands: Dejac, Appynite

  • Emcure Pharmaceuticals Ltd. & Zuventus Healthcare

    • Flagship Brands: Sustinex (Emcure), Ejalong (Zuventus)

  • Macleods Pharmaceuticals Ltd.

    • Flagship Brand: Kutub

  • Ajanta Pharma Ltd.

    • Flagship Brand: Super Kamagra (Combines Sildenafil 100 mg + Dapoxetine 60 mg)

  • Akumentis Healthcare Ltd.

    • Flagship Brand: Duraject

2. Specialty Export & High-Volume Generic Formulation Leaders

These companies specialize in sexual health and wellness formulations, producing finished oral solid dosage forms (tablets, film-coated, and soft chewables) for both white-label export and their own global brand labels:

  • Sunrise Remedies Pvt. Ltd. (Gujarat)

    • Standalone Brand: Poxet (Poxet-30, Poxet-60, Poxet-90)

    • Combo Brands: Super P-Force (with Sildenafil), Super Tadarise (with Tadalafil), Super Avana (with Avanafil)

  • Centurion Laboratories Pvt. Ltd. (Gujarat)

    • Standalone Brand: Vriligy (60 mg)

    • Combo Brands: Cenforce-D (with Sildenafil), Super Vidalista (with Tadalafil)

  • Fortune Healthcare Pvt. Ltd. (Gujarat)

    • Standalone Brand: Duratia (30 mg, 60 mg, 90 mg)

    • Combo Brands: Fildena Extra Super, Filagra DXT

  • Healing Pharma Pvt. Ltd. (Maharashtra)

    • Standalone Brand: Dapoforce (30 mg, 60 mg, 90 mg)

  • Taj Pharma India Ltd. (Mumbai)

    • Standalone Brand: Dapoxetine HCl Taj Pharma (30 mg & 60 mg WHO-GMP generic export packs)
